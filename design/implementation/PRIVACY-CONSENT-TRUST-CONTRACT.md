# Privacy, Consent, Trust & External-Source Contract
CareerScope must maintain explicit controls for recruiter/profile visibility, resume visibility,
contact permission, optional AI processing, optional interview recording/transcription, analytics
preferences, notification preferences, consent history and withdrawal where applicable.

External providers:
- Record provider/source provenance and permitted integration mode.
- Respect applicable provider terms, robots/access controls, rate limits, authentication boundaries
  and licensing/content restrictions.
- Never bypass CAPTCHAs, access controls or login walls; never steal/replay user sessions or cookies.
- Canonical employer/ATS links are preferred where legitimately available.
- Unknown verification/provenance values remain unknown.

Privacy/security:
- Collect only necessary data for declared purposes; use retention/deletion policies and access controls.
- Encrypt sensitive data in transit and at rest where appropriate.
- Redact secrets and unnecessary PII from logs.
- Data export/deletion are authenticated, authorized and audited workflows.
- Generated screenshots saying “compliant” are illustrative only. Legal compliance/certification is
  not established by UI design and requires jurisdiction-specific implementation/review.
