## Important source-asset limitation
The archive contains all approved image references available from this design process, but it does NOT contain genuine isolated vector/source exports for every visual element. In particular:
- exact standalone CareerScope logo SVG variants
- complete favicon/ICO export set
- isolated mountain/path artwork source vectors
- every individual icon SVG
- official third-party company/provider logo packs

These must not be fabricated and labeled as original source assets. Codex should use the canonical screenshots plus the icon mapping and perform screenshot-diff validation. Official third-party marks should use legally permitted official assets.

## Final UX/Foundation closure
The final foundation boards and contracts now cover global search, notifications, help/support,
account security, privacy/consent, job trust/safety, component states, responsive behavior,
accessibility, localization readiness, route/permission/feature-flag modeling, transactional UX,
data export/deletion and audit presentation.

Design coverage is complete for the currently defined CareerScope product vision. This is not a
claim that implementation, legal compliance, production telemetry, third-party integrations or
future unknown product requirements are complete.
