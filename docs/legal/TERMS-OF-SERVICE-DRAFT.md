# CareerScope Terms of Service — Counsel Draft

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED, NOT A LEGAL OPINION, AND MUST NOT BE PUBLISHED OR
USED TO ENABLE REGISTRATION.** This draft is based on repository behavior and
approved CareerScope product boundaries. Counsel and the owner must complete the
review checklist before a version is approved or configured.

## 1. Service

CareerScope is a candidate-owned workspace for organizing career profile
information, discovering eligible job listings, reviewing deterministic
matching evidence, saving opportunities, and recording application activity.
The service does not submit job applications for a candidate. Opening an
employer or job-source link does not mean an application was submitted or change
its recorded lifecycle status.

## 2. Accounts and access

Account creation is available only when the operator has enabled the approved
email-verification and registration configuration. A verification code is
short-lived and must not be shared. Users are responsible for protecting access
to their email account and for promptly reporting suspected unauthorized use.
Existing account access is not retroactively conditioned on this draft.

## 3. User content and accuracy

Users control information they submit to their profile, preferences, resume,
saved-job notes, and application records. Users should have authority to submit
that content. Users remain responsible for reviewing information before using
it outside CareerScope. CareerScope does not represent that third-party job
information is complete, current, available, or suitable for a particular user.

## 4. Job sources and external destinations

CareerScope may display information obtained from separately authorized job
sources. Source identity and provenance are shown where available. External
websites are operated independently and may have their own terms, privacy
notices, eligibility rules, and application processes. Users decide whether to
visit or apply through an external destination.

## 5. Acceptable use

Users must not interfere with service security, access another person's data,
misuse credentials, bypass source restrictions or rate limits, or use the
service to conduct unlawful or abusive activity. This draft does not authorize
automated application submission, credential or session scraping, CAPTCHA
bypass, or collection from sources without permission.

## 6. Availability and changes

CareerScope may change or suspend functionality to protect users, comply with
law, or maintain the service. Availability commitments, notice requirements,
and any service-level terms require owner and counsel approval; no uptime
guarantee is made by this draft.
